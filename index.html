How a citation could break an unrelated menu

Empty converted cards

Legacy DEEP_RESEARCH sources produced no converted citation cards in the inspected frontend.

A rendering exception

A template attempted to read the absent first card. An exception interrupted scheduled work.

A stranded queue

An expired callback handle remained set. Later work, including menu loading, was left waiting.

The Angular scheduler failure also reproduces without a Gemini account, browser, or AdGuard. The Gemini rendering finding is specific to the inspected September environment.

A workaround, with a clear limit

The v1.2.0 userscript omits only empty legacy Deep Research citation widgets and recovers the existing queue after a successful application tick. It sends no data and stores no conversation content. It depends on internal frontend structures and can stop matching later builds.

This is a local compatibility workaround, not an official Google repair. The earlier version already required revision after Gemini changed internal identifiers.
