# Resolution criteria and owner actions

## Gemini

- Handle legacy Deep Research sources without dereferencing an absent converted card.
- Keep a citation-rendering error from leaving unrelated attachment controls permanently unloaded.
- Verify the affected existing-conversation path with the local script disabled before a fresh load.
- Confirm repeated menu opening and a harmless unsent attachment on the repaired build.
- Record the test date and available deployment identity. State any environment that remains untested.

A working Android comparison, a successful new conversation, or a local userscript does not establish resolution of the original Safari web-app failure.

## Angular

The [post-merge follow-up](https://github.com/angular/angular/issues/70728#issuecomment-5721025628) asks for the remaining scheduler issue to be reopened or tracked separately. An exception-safe cleanup should clear an expired in-flight handle, preserve error propagation and re-entrancy protection, and allow fresh work after both callback and application-tick failures. Both idle-deadline and timeout-fallback modes need coverage.

The pinned source reproduction demonstrates the remaining failure at specific historical commits. Any later source change needs its own test; even a passing Angular test would not establish Gemini deployment.

## Status discipline

Keep these states distinct: reported, acknowledged, patch merged, tested at a revision, deployed, and verified in the affected application. Close this tracker only with a clearly dated result and the limits of that result.
